Synesthesia

Game Overview

Synesthesia is a browser-based puzzle game where you are presented with a grid of colored tiles, each representing a different color. Your goal is to match adjacent tiles in the same color group by sliding them around.

Controls and Objective

The game is controlled using the mouse. You can drag and drop tiles from one row to another. The objective is to create groups of three or more tiles of the same color, earning points for each successful match.

The game consists of a large grid of 20x20 tiles, divided into 16x16 sub-grids. You can move your tiles up, down, left, or right by dragging them from one cell to another.

Tips and Tricks for Success

Here are some tips to help you improve your gameplay:

  • Focus on creating color groups in the center of the grid first. This will give you a solid foundation for building bigger groups later.
  • Use your tiles strategically, trying to create bigger groups and combos as soon as possible.
  • Keep an eye out for opportunities to swap tiles between adjacent rows or columns.
  • Don't be afraid to block an opponent's move if it would leave you with no options.

Why You Should Play It

Synesthesia is a challenging but rewarding game that requires strategy and quick thinking. By playing the game regularly, you can improve your skills and get better at solving puzzles. Additionally, the game's unique blend of color-matching mechanics and spatial reasoning makes it accessible to players of all ages and skill levels.

Gameplay Loop

The gameplay loop consists of two main phases: drawing and building.

In the drawing phase, you are presented with a new row or column of tiles. You can choose which tile to place in that spot by clicking on it.

In the building phase, you can move existing tiles around to create groups of three or more tiles of the same color. When you earn enough points for a group, you win the round and get to start drawing again.
